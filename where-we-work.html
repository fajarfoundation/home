Where we work

We work where we come from: rural Maharashtra.

Government tribal residential schools in Melghat, Amravati district, with more schools to come.

Now

Melghat, Amravati district

Melghat is a forested, largely tribal region in the Satpura hills of northern Amravati district. We work in two government tribal residential (ashram) schools here, with students in Classes 9 to 12.

2schools
183students
9–12classes
Why we started here. In Maharashtra, Scheduled Tribe students drop out of Classes 9 and 10 at almost twice the overall rate.

Achalpur tahsil and the Melghat region, Amravati district. Map © OpenStreetMap contributors.

Our office: At Tawlar, Pathrot, Achalpur City, Amravati - 444808, Maharashtra

Next: Beed district

We plan to extend the programme to schools in Beed district in Marathwada, where many students face the same lack of guidance at the same decision points.

How we work with schools

We work inside government schools, with the permission and support of school leadership and education officials.

Teachers stay involved

Sessions run during school time, and we involve teachers so the work can continue.

If you are a headteacher, education officer or local organisation in rural Maharashtra, we would like to hear from you.
