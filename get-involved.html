Support our work

Help more rural students choose their future.

There are four ways to be part of it. Write to us and we will take it from there.

Fund the programme

Our school work is currently unfunded and run by volunteers. Funding would let us hire a dedicated facilitator and reach more schools. Write to us and we will share our budget and plan.

Ask for our budget →

Partner with us

Foundations, companies and government departments can partner with us on programme delivery, research or expansion to new districts.

Propose a partnership →

Mentor a student

Commit an hour a month, by phone or video, to guide a student through college choices and the first years after school.

Offer to mentor →

Talk about your career

Students rarely meet people outside a few familiar jobs. A 30-minute talk about your work, online or in person, can open a door.

Offer a career talk →
